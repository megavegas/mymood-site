mymood

See who is around. Make a group. Set a time.

mymood is a free app for Android phones. You introduce yourself, you choose the distance, and you see people who have the app open at that moment. If you both want to, you talk. You can also make a group and set a meeting for that group.

Version 0.1.20 · Free · For ages 18 and over

What it does

  • A short introduction: your name, a few lines about you, and tastes such as travel, sport, books, music, films, and what you can do.
  • People nearby, inside a distance you choose.
  • A moment, with words or a photo, for people around you. It stays only on the phones that receive it.
  • A request to talk. The chat does not start until the other person accepts.
  • A group by invite. Someone without the code does not join.
  • A meeting for a group or for a two-person chat, with a reminder on your phone.

How you use it

  1. Download the file and install it. If the phone asks, allow install from this browser.
  2. Choose a language: English, Persian, or Arabic.
  3. Write your name and introduction, and confirm you are 18 or older.
  4. Set the distance and refresh the list. Strangers do not get your exact pin.
  5. If you see someone, send a request. Talking starts after they accept.
  6. For a group, create one and give the invite code only to those people.
  7. For a meeting, set a title and an hour. Your phone reminds you when it is close.

A few plain notes

In a chat, if you both turn on live place for each other, that page shows where you are and how close you get. Until both turn it on, nobody sees a precise place.

You can open the nearby range up to 200 km. A group and a meeting are made on their own pages. You do not need to find someone first.

If you want to be seen more, send a pulse from the nearby page. For twenty minutes you show above the others.

If two of you want to see each other, type the same word on the Around page. Keep place on, and leave the mymood notice up. While a new file is saving, the top of the app says so and shows how far the download has got.

On Windows, open the zip and run mymood. You can start on the computer alone, or save a backup from You on the phone and open that file on the computer so the introduction stays the same.

The log is on the phone in You, and on Windows it is the Log tab.

If the phone and the computer share the same home internet, the introduction also travels inside that home. A chat request shows on the phone there too.

Walking to each other is on the chat page. Both turn on live place. Back stays inside the app. While the mymood notice is on the phone, place keeps going outside the app too.

From the You page, save a backup file. If you ever remove the app, bring that file back after you install again. When an update sits on the same app, you do not remove it.

Both people need the app open to see each other. Your introduction stays on your phone. We do not make an account or a user list. If a VPN is already on, mymood uses that path.
